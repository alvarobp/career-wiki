- Invisible hierarchies
- Rank
    - Perception of legitimacy
    - Icons of power (symbolic violence, social/cultural capital) - You are blind to your privilege
    - Power is given
- Stereotypes
    - Help making sense of situations with hierarchies, ranking...
- Ranking + Stereotypes + Power lead to Cognitive Biases
    - I'll go with his opinion cause his the expert, the powerful one...
- Rank and Hierarchy are not necessarily bad, they may help, but of course they may and many times lead to biases, injustice...
- Book: Humble Inquiry

    - Give space
    - Ask people to ask questions
    - People need to feel safe
        - Very hard, if not impossible, for a facilitator/single person to achieve (usually there's distrust in the group)
