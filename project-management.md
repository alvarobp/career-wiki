# Project Management

> Project Management is the act of breaking a complex end goal down into smaller pieces, putting those pieces in roughly the most effective order they should be done, identifying which pieces can be done in parallel and which must be done in sequence, and attempting to tease out the unknowns of the project that may cause it to slow down or fail completely.
> 

[The Manager’s Path - Camille Fournier](https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/)

### **Thinkie: One Pass Quickly, One Pass Good**

> Pattern: You have a project that requires many fairly independent pieces.
> 
> 
> Transformation: Radically scale back your ambitions for the pieces & deliver all of them in a first pass, then go through all the pieces & improve them.
> 

[Thinkie: One Pass Quickly, One Pass Good - Kent Beck](https://tidyfirst.substack.com/p/thinkie-one-pass-quickly-one-pass)
