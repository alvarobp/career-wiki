[Johari Window](../personal-growth/johari-window.md) — feedback is how you shrink your blind spots: the things others see in you that you can't. Ask 2–3 people you trust what you do that you might not be aware of, trust a pattern across several of them more than a single comment, and ask when there's time to take it in rather than right before a high-stakes moment. (From [Untools: Why other people can see things about you that you can't](https://buttondown.com/untools/archive/why-other-people-can-see-things-about-you-that/))
