[Monorepo Explained](https://monorepo.tools/)

[The Home Repo Pattern](../ai/coding/techniques/home-repo-pattern.md) — the fallback when a monorepo isn't possible (a mix of public and private repos, or no political will to merge them): one repo holds the skills, steering files and tooling, and agents run from it to work across all the other repos. From [Matt Pocock's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWe-j78vNpc).
