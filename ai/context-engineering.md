# Context Engineering

Context engineering quickly becomes a core skill for anyone working with large language models (LLMs) and AI agents. Unlike prompt engineering, which is about crafting single questions or requests, context engineering focuses on providing the right background, files, and environment so LLMs can solve your task. [https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/nlw/episodes/Context-Engineering-What-It-Is-and-Why-It-Matters-e34o948](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/nlw/episodes/Context-Engineering-What-It-Is-and-Why-It-Matters-e34o948)

> Context Engineering is the discipline of designing and building dynamic systems that provides the right information and tools, in the right format, at the right time, to give a LLM everything it needs to accomplish a task.
