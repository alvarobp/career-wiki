# Lesson 4: How to Suck at AI Coding: Common Pitfalls and Practical Solutions

**Transition to Intermediate AI Coding**

- The lesson marks a transition from fundamentals to intermediate concepts in AI coding.
- Focus on understanding where AI coding can fail and how to resolve common issues.

**Importance of the Big Three**

- Emphasis on balancing the "big three" components: prompt, model, and context.
- Identify and avoid common pitfalls that can hinder performance and productivity.

**Principle for the Lesson**

- "Balance, then boost": priority on doing it correctly before improving efficiency.
- Focus on aligning prompts, context, and models in AI coding.

**Common AI Coding Pitfalls**

1. **Context Issues**:
    - **Missing Context**: Often leads to incomplete or incorrect outputs.
    - **Excessive Context**: Overloading the model with too much information can confuse it, resulting in errors.
2. **Prompt Quality**:
    - Prompts can either be too high-level (lacking detail) or too low-level (overly detailed).
    - The sweet spot is mid-level prompts which give clear directions.
3. **Model Selection**:
    - Choosing weaker models can make coding inefficient and prone to errors.
    - Stronger models (e.g., GPT-3.5 or newer) provide more accurate outputs.
