**Idea watching the video from 👆🏻**

When starting a product/company, before market fit, invest on white-box testing (unit and integration tests) do black-box testing (end2end critial paths/regression tests) manually by maintaining the list of critical paths to test on a spreadsheet or similar. Once passed market-fit and/or the impact of a bug on a critical path is crucial then automate black-box tests.
