# Cover Letter

Hi folks,

My name is Álvaro Bautista and I’m from Valencia, Spain. Applying for a job at 37Signals has been on my mind for, ever. I finally decided to take a chance! After many years of working at a few startups I got fed up with politics, overly complex technologies and, let’s be honest boredom a lot of the time. So, I am now conviced that a small dedicated caring team is what best suites the next chapter in this side of my life.

I’ve got experience building, maintaining and operating web based software products, from their early stages. I enjoy all parts of what it means to build software products. Even though my expertise is more grounded in everything in between, I enjoy a good design discussion as much as I love joining SREs and do “less flashy” crucial work. It is so exciting when all the pieces are put to work together and I can see people using and finding valuable what I’ve contributed to build. 

Regardless of the nature of the work, I put a lot of care on how it is done. I don’t mind changing my opinion after a properly argumented discussion. I don’t mind trying new approaches out. I also don’t mind speaking when I think something is not adequate or I think it deserves some improvement. I think there is no absolute good or wrong in what we do, but instead, I frame options as more or less appropriate. I prefer simple to complex. I prefer less sooner than tons later. I’ve grown to try hard to be thoughtful instead of holding onto all the dogma that surrounds us. I could make so many other statements, but I hope these convey enough about how I approach our craft.

I’ve been working with Ruby and Ruby on Rails since 2007. I’ve been part of a team that built a browser heavy React + TypeScript application for the last 5 years. It was a great learning experience and all, but oh my. I rejoined a couple of old times colleagues a few months ago to help them grow their product, which is built with Ruby on Rails. I’ve grown to think tech is not the most important thing, that what matters is the product, the outcomes... But at the same time, when I code in Ruby, I feel the joy of being comfortably at home.

The fact that you shared Ruby on Rails with all of us so long ago is what made me think programming could be a happy career for me in the first place. For that I’m very grateful.

I truly believe I would be a great addition to 37Signals team as a focused thoughtful and caring programmer.
