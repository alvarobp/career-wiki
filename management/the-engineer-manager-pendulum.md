Tags: Career, Management

[The Engineer/Manager Pendulum](https://charity.wtf/2017/05/11/the-engineer-manager-pendulum/)

Summary: They should not be mutually exclusive forever. Being a Manager and an Engineer cannot be done at the same time effectively, yes. However, the fact that you become a Manager on a team does not mean you have to be a manager forever on your career. Charity has already gone from engineer to manager and back to engineer and then again manager a few times in her career. That's made her a better engineer (tech lead) and also a better manager.
